## Compressed Content

The fundamental paradigm shift from user-driven search to AI-driven selection is occurring through the proliferation of multi-agent AI systems, the standardization of MCP and A2A protocols, and the emergence of agentic commerce (p. 1-180), necessitating a new strategy called AI Search Optimization (AIEO) to thrive in this era where AI's selection power takes precedence over human agency (p. 228-260).

## Core Concepts

### Multi-Agent AI Systems
AI agents are systems that understand user requests and autonomously complete tasks using tools or external services (p. 21-24), with the latest evolution being "multi-agent systems" where multiple agents collaborate like teams. For example, when tasked with increasing productivity in an advertising firm, a project manager AI delegates roles to specialized agents including market research AI, copywriting AI, visual design AI, and others, enabling AI to make decisions and execute complex strategies autonomously without human intervention (p. 39-46).

### MCP and A2A: AI Interconnection Technologies
MCP (Model Context Protocol), announced by Anthropic in November 2024, functions like a USB-C port, enabling AI to connect to other programs and services in a standardized manner (p. 62-66). A2A (Agent2Agent) protocol provides the method for AI agents to communicate and collaborate with each other, similar to how the internet enables communication between computers, allowing AI systems worldwide to interact seamlessly (p. 77-81). With the official launch of the A2A protocol by the Linux Foundation in June 2025, standardization and interoperability among global AI agents are accelerating (p. 94-99).

### Agentic Commerce Revolution
The era has begun where AI agents handle all stages of online shopping (p. 119-121). Observing Amazon's Alexa auto-ordering and subscription optimization reveals how AI autonomy is expanding progressively (p. 128-131). The digital advertising market is also transforming, shifting from a paradigm of exposure and click-inducement to one focused on facilitating AI clicks (p. 132-136).

### Zero Click Era
Zero Click refers to a significant decline in user clicking behavior, as users increasingly obtain information without additional clicks thanks to AI-based search engines (p. 187-198). Following Google's AI Overview implementation, click-through rates (CTR) decreased by 30% or more in most analyses, with some data showing over 50% decline by late 2025, as users view only AI-provided summaries and cease navigation to other sites (p. 194-198).

### AI Search Optimization (AIEO) Imperative
The evolution from Search Engine Optimization (SEO) to AI Search Optimization (AIEO) is underway (p. 228-236), where the frequency and accuracy of AI citation of information becomes paramount. Organizations must create clear, concise content that AI can easily comprehend and cite, while leveraging structured data such as schema markup (p. 245-248).

### Search Paradigm Transformation

The most significant distinction between traditional Google search and generative AI search lies in their response methods (p. 201-209). Google searches provided only indirect opinions through reports and recommendation videos, placing the burden of information discovery and judgment on users. In contrast, generative AI like ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Gemini provide direct answers to user questions, producing effects similar to strong YouTube recommendations (p. 207-209).

AI response accuracy continues to improve, as ChatGPT Search, Google's AI Overview, and Perplexity combine real-time web search with increasingly accurate answers (p. 221-223). However, this convenience simultaneously increases user dependency on AI judgment (p. 225-226).

### Multi-Agent AI and Collaboration Technologies

Two core technological innovations enable AI agents to handle complex tasks (p. 47-49). First, the evolution of human-like reasoning abilities has granted AI the capacity for step-by-step thinking, known as "Chain-of-Thought" capability (p. 50-52). OpenAI's O1 model exemplifies this advancement, with reasoning models significantly enhancing AI agents' ability to systematically process complex tasks, self-check answers, and handle unexpected situations (p. 53-57).

Second, advancing connectivity technologies enable AI systems to communicate with each other and utilize external tools (p. 58-61). No matter how sophisticated individual AI systems are, they require connection to internal company data and external services to truly provide value (p. 59-61).

A concrete A2A protocol example illustrates this: when asked to "find a Python developer in New York and schedule an interview," the main AI receives the request, delegates to a specialist job-finding AI, which searches multiple job platforms to create a candidate list, after which the main AI requests the scheduling AI to arrange interviews, with each AI focusing on their specialty and communicating seamlessly through A2A (p. 84-93).

Gartner forecasts that by 2026, 40% of enterprise applications will incorporate task-specialized AI agents (p. 100-101), representing an 8x increase from current 5% levels. According to PitchBook, 156 AI agent-related startups received venture investment in 2024, with funding reaching $8.2 billion, an 81.4% increase year-over-year (p. 102-105).

### Emergence of the Agent Economy

Sequoia Capital analyzes that AI model reasoning advancement is spawning new agent applications, with paradigm shift from "Software-as-a-Service" to "Service-as-a-Software" (p. 107-112). The market size for this sector is projected to exceed $10 trillion (p. 113).

Public cloud companies project entry into the "AI Agent Economy Era," with predictions that big tech will invest over $1 trillion in this domain (p. 114-117).

### AI-Driven Shopping Revolution

As AI agents become mainstream, smartphones will increasingly understand user context (p. 122-126). Future shopping AI agents may research and purchase desired apples based solely on the word "apple," having learned user preferences for apple varieties.

Currently, AI primarily collects, compares, and recommends products while humans make purchase decisions (p. 127-129), though AI autonomy gradually expands, suggesting that purchases without human intervention approach inevitability (p. 130-131).

Agentic commerce emergence may fundamentally transform the digital advertising market itself (p. 132-136). Traditional advertising focused on banner exposure and click inducement; now facilitating AI clicks becomes paramount.

Perplexity is conducting related experiments, beginning to display AI-recommended products in shopping features (p. 144-146). For example, when asked "Which running shoes are best?", AI might place higher-paying advertisers in positions 1 or 2 among five recommendations (p. 148-150).

Concerns about this practice are growing, with increasing demands for AI recommendation system transparency (p. 151-152). The EU has mandated algorithm transparency reports through the Digital Services Act (DSA), with similar regulations likely applying to AI-based recommendations (p. 152-154).

### The Era of Being Selected by AI

Deloitte forecasts that 25% of enterprises using generative AI will adopt autonomous AI agents in 2025, doubling to 50% by 2027 (p. 159-160). Amazon beta-tests "Buy for Me" functionality enabling AI to automatically order products after internet-wide searches, while payment companies like PayPal and Visa develop technology for AI agents to safely conduct automatic transactions (p. 161-163).

AI agents outperform human shopping due to overwhelming speed and rational judgment capabilities (p. 165-166). Remarkably, AI agents negotiate with each other, meaning your shopping AI agent may automatically negotiate prices with vendor AIs to achieve better deals—no longer science fiction (p. 167-168).

This transformation shifts consumer behavior from active searching to passive delegation, with enterprises facing new challenges: implementing AAO (AI Agent Optimization) to structure product information for AI comprehension (p. 170-173).

AI agents fundamentally transform how we live, with everything changing from how we seek information to how we make purchases (p. 175-176).

### AIEO Concrete Strategies

AI-based search service algorithms are evolving; previously recommending Naver blogs extensively, they now prioritize authoritative websites, adjusting algorithms so higher-credibility sources receive greater exposure (p. 241-244).

AI particularly prioritizes content demonstrating strong E-E-A-T (Experience, Expertise, Authority, Trustworthiness) principles (p. 245-246). Organizations must enhance content authority and trustworthiness while creating clear, concise material that AI easily understands and cites, leveraging structured data like schema markup (p. 246-248).

The MarTech industry sees rapid emergence of AI search optimization specialized tools, from AI content generation tools like Jasper and Copy.ai to Surfer SEO's AI optimization features, with technical solutions for creating AI-optimized content advancing quickly (p. 249-254).

### Human-AI Symbiosis

Amid these transformations, new opportunities emerge; companies providing brand storytelling and personalized experiences maintain direct customer relationships (p. 255-256). Business models differentiating through human touch, real-time communication, and customized services unavailable from AI also emerge (p. 257-258).

Enterprises pursue parallel strategies, building unique brand stories and communities for direct customer engagement rather than relying solely on AI optimization (p. 308-309). Ultimately, AI serves as a tool enhancing efficiency and information reliability, while preserving and advancing distinctly human emotional and creative domains (p. 310-312).

Finding balance amid change requires moving beyond researching AI selection techniques to providing genuinely valuable information and services through AI while maintaining human experience and creativity (p. 325-327).
